(a) Authorization.—Subject to the availability of appropriations, the Administrator shall establish and administer a Pilot Program to—

(b) Selection Criteria.—In evaluating proposals, the Administrator shall consider—

  (2) technical feasibility and scalability;

(c) Term; Sunset.—

SECTION 5. REPORTING, REVIEW, AND RULEMAKING AUTHORITY.  
(a) Interim and Final Reports.—

(b) Rulemaking.—Upon receipt of the final report, the Administrator is authorized to promulgate or revise regulations under the Clean Air Act (42 U.S.C. 7401 et seq.) or other applicable statutes to integrate successful Emerging Technologies into routine permitting, standards, or incentive programs.
