(b) Traditional approaches to addressing climate change, while valuable, may not be sufficient to meet the urgent and evolving challenges.
(c) Emerging scientific methods and technologies offer promising new pathways to mitigate and adapt to climate change.

(a) Collaboration
The Act encourages the formation of public-private partnerships to leverage resources and expertise in advancing climate innovation.
(b) Incentives
